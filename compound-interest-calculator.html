The Mathematics of Compound Interest

Compound interest is calculated using the standard formula:

A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt) + PMT × [((1 + r/n)^(nt) - 1) / (r/n)]

Where A is the final balance, P is the principal, r is the interest rate, n is compounding frequency, t is time in years, and PMT is recurring contributions.
